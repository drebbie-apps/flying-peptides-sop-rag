# QMS-003: Control of nonconforming product

**Positive Climb Diagnostics · Standard Operating Procedure**

*Flying Peptides training case · Fictitious manufacturer: Positive Climb Diagnostics · Not a real company, product or QMS.*

*Names and roles are illustrative (training case); all roles were performed by Dr. Ebbie. Drafted with AI assistance; reviewed and approved by Dr. Ebbie.*

## 1 Purpose

This procedure defines how nonconforming product, process steps and documents are identified, segregated, recorded, evaluated, dispositioned and closed, so that nonconforming product is not used or released unintentionally (ISO 13485 §8.3).

## 2 Scope

### 2.1 Applicability

Nonconformities of three kinds: product (incoming materials and reagents such as antibody-bead batches and calibrators, in-process material, assay runs and released results); process (a step of a controlled procedure not performed as written); and documents (QMS-001 §7.2.3).

### 2.2 Qualifications and training

All staff are trained on this SOP before working under the QMS, because anyone may detect a nonconformity. Dispositions and concessions are decided only by Quality Assurance.

### 2.3 Exclusions

Complaints from users, the reporting of serious incidents and advisory notices are handled under QMS-007 (Complaint handling, vigilance and advisory notices); this procedure only links to them (QMS-003 §6.5.2). Root-cause analysis and corrective action are handled under QMS-004; this procedure ends with the decision whether CAPA is needed.

## 3 Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| :---- | :---- |
| Person who detects it | Any trained staff member: stops the affected activity, segregates affected product and raises the NCR. |
| Associate Scientist | Carries out the decided disposition and any rework according to an approved procedure. |
| Technical Reviewer | Assesses the extent and rates the risk of the nonconformity, proposes a disposition, assesses possible adverse effects of rework and re-verifies reworked product. |
| Quality Assurance | Owns the process; assigns NCR numbers; decides on investigation, disposition, concessions, external notification and CAPA; sets and ends product status; closes NCRs; trends nonconformities. |

## 6 Procedure

### 6.1 Identification and segregation

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Person who detects it | 6.1.1 Stop the affected activity. Do not use the affected product, result or document. |
| Person who detects it | 6.1.2 Label affected physical items with QMS-003-F02, or set the status of electronic items and results to Quarantine in the electronic QMS. |
| Person who detects it | 6.1.3 Move labelled physical items to the quarantine area for their storage condition, as listed in the storage location list of the electronic QMS [ADDED FOR TRAINING]. |
| Person who detects it | 6.1.4 Raise a nonconformity record (QMS-003-F01) within the time limit of QMS-003 §7.1.1. State what was found, the requirement not met (document ID and section) and the affected items (batch, plate, run or document ID). |
| Quality Assurance | 6.1.5 Assign the NCR number and confirm that the affected items are segregated. |

### 6.2 Evaluation

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Technical Reviewer | 6.2.1 Assess the extent: other batches, plates, runs or documents affected, and whether results already released are affected. |
| Technical Reviewer | 6.2.2 Rate the risk to patient results and to product performance as low, medium or high on the scale of the risk management file RMF-001 (ISO 14971:2019) [ADDED FOR TRAINING]. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.2.3 Decide whether an investigation is needed, and record the decision and the reason in the NCR (ISO 13485 §8.3.1). Investigate at least when the cause is unknown or the risk is rated high [ADDED FOR TRAINING]. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.2.4 Decide whether an external party responsible for the nonconformity, such as the supplier of a material, must be informed. Record the decision and the reason (ISO 13485 §8.3.1). Users of released results are informed under QMS-003 §6.5.1; authorities under QMS-003 §6.5.2. |

### 6.3 Disposition

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Technical Reviewer | 6.3.1 Propose a disposition in the NCR, with the reason: no nonconformity confirmed, rework, use under concession, scrap or return to supplier. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.3.2 [QA SIGN-OFF] Decide the disposition. Record the decision, the reason and the identity of the person who authorised it. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.3.3 Accept a concession only with a documented justification and when regulatory requirements are still met (ISO 13485 §8.3.2). |
| Quality Assurance | 6.3.4 [QA SIGN-OFF] Set the status and record it in the NCR: Released for "no nonconformity confirmed" or "use under concession"; Rejected for scrap or return to supplier. Product for rework stays in Quarantine until QMS-003 §6.4.4. |
| Associate Scientist | 6.3.5 Carry out the disposition: remove the quarantine label from Released items, or destroy or return Rejected items. Record it in the NCR. |

### 6.4 Rework and re-verification

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Associate Scientist | 6.4.1 Rework only according to a procedure that was reviewed and approved in the same way as the original procedure (QMS-001 §6.3, §6.4; ISO 13485 §8.3.4), e.g. re-injection under TM-104 §7.6.9. |
| Technical Reviewer | 6.4.2 Before rework starts, assess possible adverse effects of the rework on the product and record the assessment in the NCR (ISO 13485 §8.3.4). |
| Technical Reviewer | 6.4.3 Re-verify the reworked product against the applicable acceptance criteria and regulatory requirements, and record the result in the NCR (ISO 13485 §8.3.4). |
| Quality Assurance | 6.4.4 [QA SIGN-OFF] Release reworked product only after successful re-verification, and set its status to Released. If re-verification fails, decide a new disposition (QMS-003 §6.3.2). |

### 6.5 Nonconformities found after release

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Quality Assurance | 6.5.1 If nonconforming product or results have already been released, take action appropriate to the effects, e.g. a correction of the released results or a recall, and inform the users (ISO 13485 §8.3.3). |
| Quality Assurance | 6.5.2 Assess whether the event is a serious incident or needs a field safety corrective action under IVDR Article 82, or whether an advisory notice must be issued (ISO 13485 §8.3.3), and act according to QMS-007 (Complaint handling, vigilance and advisory notices). |

### 6.6 Process and document nonconformities

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Person who detects it | 6.6.1 Record a process step not performed as written, or a nonconforming controlled document, on QMS-003-F01 (e.g. a Superseded SOP found at a point of use). |
| Quality Assurance | 6.6.2 Assess whether product or results made under the nonconforming process or document are affected. Handle nonconforming product as per QMS-003 §6.1 to §6.5. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.6.3 For a document nonconformity, inform the document's author so that a change request is raised (QMS-001 §6.2.1). |

### 6.7 CAPA decision and closure

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Quality Assurance | 6.7.1 Decide whether CAPA is needed (QMS-004). Open CAPA at least when (a) the risk is rated high (QMS-003 §6.2.2); (b) released results are affected (QMS-003 §6.5); or (c) the nonconformity recurs, i.e. the same requirement (same document ID and section) is not met for the same product or process at least twice within 12 months [ADDED FOR TRAINING]. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.7.2 [QA SIGN-OFF] Close the NCR when the disposition is complete, the final status is set (QMS-003 §6.3.4 or §6.4.4) and any CAPA number is linked, within the time limit of QMS-003 §7.1.2. |

### 6.8 Process overview

Detect → stop, quarantine and segregate → record (NCR, QMS-003-F01) → evaluate extent, risk, need for investigation and external notification → disposition (no nonconformity confirmed, rework, use under concession, scrap or return to supplier) → set status Released or Rejected; for rework: assess adverse effects, rework, re-verify, release → CAPA decision (QMS-004) → close. Nonconformities found after release follow QMS-003 §6.5; process and document nonconformities follow QMS-003 §6.6.

## 7 Process controls, nonconformities and records

### 7.1 Decision criteria and timelines

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Person who detects it | 7.1.1 Raise the NCR within 1 working day of detection. |
| Quality Assurance | 7.1.2 Close each NCR within 30 calendar days of detection, or record the reason for the extension in the NCR. |
| Quality Assurance | 7.1.3 Trend nonconformities by type, process and product every quarter and report the trend to management review. |
| All staff | 7.1.4 Never use or release product with status Quarantine or Rejected. |

### 7.2 Nonconforming process execution

If a step of this procedure cannot be performed as written, or a time limit in QMS-003 §7.1 is missed:

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Person who detects it | 7.2.1 Record it on QMS-003-F01 as a process nonconformity (QMS-003 §6.6). Segregation of affected product (QMS-003 §6.1.2, §6.1.3) always continues. |
| Quality Assurance | 7.2.2 Evaluate it and decide on CAPA as for any other nonconformity (QMS-003 §6.2, §6.7.1). |

### 7.3 Control of records (ISO 13485 §4.2.5)

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Person who detects it | 7.3.1 Record the description, the affected items and the immediate actions in the NCR at the time of detection. |
| Technical Reviewer | 7.3.2 Record the extent, the risk rating, the proposed disposition, the assessment of possible adverse effects of rework and any re-verification results in the NCR. |
| Quality Assurance | 7.3.3 Record the decision whether to investigate and its reason, any investigation and its result, the disposition and the identity of the person who authorised it, any concession and its justification, status changes, corrections, notifications and closure in the NCR (ISO 13485 §8.3.1, §8.3.2). |
| Quality Assurance | 7.3.4 Retain NCRs as quality records for the retention period set in QMS-002 (Control of records) [section to be cited when QMS-002 is drafted]. |
