# QMS-001: Document control

**Positive Climb Diagnostics · Standard Operating Procedure**

*Flying Peptides training case · Fictitious manufacturer: Positive Climb Diagnostics · Not a real company, product or QMS.*

*Names and roles are illustrative (training case); all roles were performed by Dr. Ebbie. Drafted with AI assistance; reviewed and approved by Dr. Ebbie.*

## 1 Purpose

This procedure defines how controlled documents are created, reviewed, approved, distributed, made effective, periodically reviewed, superseded and withdrawn, and how external documents are identified and distributed, so that only current, approved documents are used (ISO 13485 §4.2.4).

## 2 Scope

### 2.1 Applicability

All controlled documents of the quality management system: SOPs (QMS-0xx and TM-1xx), forms (e.g. QMS-001-F01) and templates (e.g. QMS-001-T01). External documents the QMS relies on are covered for identification and distribution only (QMS-001 §6.8).

### 2.2 Qualifications and training

Authors, reviewers and approvers are trained on this SOP before they perform any step in it. Training is recorded in the electronic QMS.

### 2.3 Exclusions

Records (completed forms, raw data, batch records) are not revised and are controlled under QMS-002 (Control of records). External documents such as standards and regulations are not authored under this SOP; their identification and distribution are controlled under QMS-001 §6.8.

## 3 Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| :---- | :---- |
| Associate Scientist (Author) | Raises change requests and names the affected roles; numbers draft versions; drafts and revises documents in the approved template; resolves review comments; trains affected staff; completes periodic reviews of own documents. |
| Technical Reviewer | Reviews drafts for technical accuracy, completeness and consistency with related SOPs; records comments; verifies each resolution; signs the technical review and the document. |
| Quality Assurance | Accepts change requests; assigns document IDs; issues major versions; approves documents as Approver; publishes Effective versions and controls copies; sets Superseded and Withdrawn status; keeps the document register, the external-document list and the periodic review schedule. |

## 4 Definitions and abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
| :---- | :---- |
| Controlled document | A document whose creation, review, approval, distribution and changes are managed under QMS-001. |
| Controlled copy | A copy whose location is recorded and that is replaced or recalled when the document changes. The electronic QMS copy is the master controlled copy. |
| External document | A document not authored by Positive Climb Diagnostics that the QMS relies on, such as a standard, a regulation or a supplier instruction. |
| Record | A document that states results achieved or provides evidence of activities performed. Records are not revised; they are controlled under QMS-002. |
| Version | The identifier of a document state: drafts 0.1, 0.2 …; first approved version 1.0; revision drafts 1.1, 1.2 …; next approved version 2.0. |
| Draft | A document being written or reviewed. Drafts must not be used for work. |
| Approver | The person whose signature releases a document for use. For all controlled documents this is Quality Assurance. |
| Approved | Status of a document signed by the Author, the Technical Reviewer and the Approver, but not yet in force. |
| Effective | Status of the approved version that is in force, from its effective date. |
| Superseded | Status of a previous version after a newer version has become Effective. Superseded versions are kept but not used. |
| Withdrawn | Status of a document taken out of use without a replacement. Withdrawn documents are kept but not used. |
| Periodic review | A scheduled check that an Effective document is still accurate, needed and compliant. |
| CAPA | Corrective and preventive action, managed under QMS-004. |

## 6 Procedure

### 6.1 Document identification and versioning

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Quality Assurance | 6.1.1 Assign each new document a unique ID: QMS-0xx for QMS SOPs, TM-1xx for method SOPs, the parent SOP ID plus -Fxx for forms (e.g. QMS-001-F01) and plus -Txx for templates (e.g. QMS-001-T01). |
| Author | 6.1.2 Number draft versions as major.minor. Start at 0.1 and add one minor step per review round (0.1, 0.2 … 0.11). |
| Quality Assurance | 6.1.3 Issue the first approved version as 1.0. Revision drafts are numbered 1.1, 1.2 …; the next approved version is 2.0. |
| Author | 6.1.4 Show the company name, title, document ID, version and "Page X of Y" in the header of every page. |

### 6.2 Drafting

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Author | 6.2.1 Raise a document change request (QMS-001-F01) that states the reason for the new document or the change, and the roles affected by it. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.2.2 Accept or reject the change request. For a new document, assign its ID (QMS-001 §6.1.1). |
| Author | 6.2.3 Write the draft in the current approved template (QMS-001 §5). Set its status to Draft. |
| Author | 6.2.4 Give every specification, limit and acceptance criterion its source (standard, validation report or publication). |
| Author | 6.2.5 Refer to other documents by ID and section, e.g. "see QMS-003 §6.2", never "as described above". |

### 6.3 Technical review

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Technical Reviewer | 6.3.1 Check the draft for technical accuracy, completeness and consistency with related SOPs. |
| Technical Reviewer | 6.3.2 Record each comment in the electronic QMS. |
| Author | 6.3.3 Resolve each comment, record the resolution and raise the minor version for the next review round (e.g. 0.1 → 0.2). |
| Technical Reviewer | 6.3.4 Verify each resolution in the new draft and set the comment to Verified closed. |
| Technical Reviewer | 6.3.5 Sign the technical review once every comment is Verified closed. |

### 6.4 QA approval

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Quality Assurance | 6.4.1 Check that the document follows QMS-001 and the approved template, and that the technical review is signed (QMS-001 §6.3.5). |
| Author | 6.4.2 Sign the document electronically as Author. |
| Technical Reviewer | 6.4.3 Sign the document electronically as Technical Reviewer. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.4.4 [QA SIGN-OFF] Sign the document electronically as Approver. With this last signature the document becomes the next major version (e.g. 1.0) with status Approved. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.4.5 Record the date of the last signature as the approval date. |

### 6.5 Training and effective date

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Quality Assurance | 6.5.1 Set the effective date to 14 calendar days after the approval date [ADDED FOR TRAINING]. |
| Author | 6.5.2 Train the staff in the roles named in the change request (QMS-001 §6.2.1) before the effective date. |
| Affected staff | 6.5.3 Complete the training and record it in the electronic QMS before working to the new version. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.5.4 [QA SIGN-OFF] Check the training status on the day before the effective date. If training is incomplete, either postpone the effective date and record the new date, or let the document become Effective with the work restricted to trained staff. |
| Affected staff | 6.5.5 If not trained by the effective date, do not perform the work covered by the document until the training is recorded. |
| All staff | 6.5.6 Do not use an Approved document before its effective date. Until then, work to the previous Effective version. |

### 6.6 Distribution, supersession and withdrawal

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Quality Assurance | 6.6.1 On the effective date, publish the Effective version in the electronic QMS. The electronic QMS copy is the master controlled copy. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.6.2 Issue paper copies for points of use only as numbered controlled copies, and record where each copy is kept. |
| All staff | 6.6.3 Treat any other printout as uncontrolled. Check it against the electronic QMS before use and do not keep it at a point of use. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.6.4 On the effective date of a new version, set the previous version to Superseded and replace its controlled paper copies. |
| Author | 6.6.5 To withdraw a document without a replacement, raise a change request (QMS-001-F01). |
| Quality Assurance | 6.6.6 Set a withdrawn document to Withdrawn and recall its controlled paper copies. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.6.7 Keep at least one copy of every Superseded and Withdrawn version in the electronic QMS for the period set in QMS-001 §7.3.5. These copies are not used for work. |

### 6.7 Periodic review

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Quality Assurance | 6.7.1 Schedule a periodic review of every Effective document at least every 2 years from its effective date [ADDED FOR TRAINING]. The 2-year period starts again when a new version becomes Effective. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.7.2 Remind the author 60 days before the review is due [ADDED FOR TRAINING]. |
| Author | 6.7.3 Confirm that the document is still accurate and needed, or start a revision (QMS-001 §6.2). |
| Quality Assurance | 6.7.4 [QA SIGN-OFF] Record the outcome: "no change", "revision needed" or "withdraw". A review without changes does not change the version number. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.7.5 Start an earlier review when a related regulation, standard, process or piece of equipment changes, or when a nonconformity or CAPA points to the document. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.7.6 If a review is overdue, keep the document Effective; work to it continues. Complete the review within 3 months of the due date [ADDED FOR TRAINING], or raise a nonconformity (QMS-003). |

### 6.8 External documents

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Quality Assurance | 6.8.1 Record every external document the QMS relies on (standards, regulations, supplier instructions) in the document register, with its edition or date. |
| Quality Assurance | 6.8.2 Make the current edition available in the electronic QMS and control its copies as for internal documents (QMS-001 §6.6). |
| Quality Assurance | 6.8.3 Check for new editions at each periodic review of the documents that cite them (QMS-001 §6.7). Inform the authors of affected documents so they can raise a change request (QMS-001 §6.2.1). |

### 6.9 Process overview

Draft (0.x) → Technical review → QA approval (1.0, Approved) → Effective (approval date + 14 calendar days, after training) → Superseded when the next version becomes Effective. Periodic review (QMS-001 §6.7) confirms the Effective version, starts a revision draft (1.1) or withdraws the document (status Withdrawn, QMS-001 §6.6).

## 7 Process controls, nonconformities and records

### 7.1 Decision criteria and timelines

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| All staff | 7.1.1 Use only Effective versions for work. Do not use Draft, Approved, Superseded or Withdrawn versions. |
| Quality Assurance | 7.1.2 Meet the time limits for the effective date (QMS-001 §6.5.1) and for periodic review (QMS-001 §6.7.1). |
| Quality Assurance | 7.1.3 Report overdue periodic reviews to management every month [ADDED FOR TRAINING]. An overdue document stays Effective (QMS-001 §6.7.6). |

### 7.2 Nonconforming process execution

If a document-control step cannot be performed as written, or a time limit set in QMS-001 §6.5.1 or §6.7.1 is missed:

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Person who detects it | 7.2.1 Stop the affected document-control activity. Work performed under Effective documents continues unless QA decides otherwise (QMS-001 §7.2.4). |
| Person who detects it | 7.2.2 Identify the affected documents and records. |
| Person who detects it | 7.2.3 Raise a nonconformity record according to QMS-003 (Control of nonconforming product), §6.6. |
| Quality Assurance | 7.2.4 Decide whether the affected work must stop and whether CAPA is needed (QMS-004). |

### 7.3 Control of records (ISO 13485 §4.2.5)

| Who | Task |
| :---- | :---- |
| Author | 7.3.1 Record each change request (QMS-001-F01) and the resolution of each review comment in the electronic QMS. |
| Technical Reviewer | 7.3.2 Record each review comment, its verification and the technical-review signature in the electronic QMS. |
| Quality Assurance | 7.3.3 Record approvals, status changes, training status and periodic review outcomes in the electronic QMS, with an audit trail of every status change. |
| Quality Assurance | 7.3.4 Retain the records of this procedure for at least 10 years after the last device covered by the documents has been placed on the market [ADDED FOR TRAINING, aligned with IVDR Art. 10(8)], and never for less than ISO 13485 §4.2.5 requires (QMS-002). |
| Quality Assurance | 7.3.5 Retain at least one copy of every Superseded or Withdrawn document for at least the lifetime of the devices made or tested under it, and never for less than the retention period of any record produced under it (ISO 13485 §4.2.4). |
